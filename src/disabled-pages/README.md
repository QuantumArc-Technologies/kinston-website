Quality, Downloads, and News are temporarily disabled. Their original page sources
are kept here, outside Astro's `src/pages` directory, so their routes and sitemap
entries are not generated.

To restore them, move `quality.astro`, `downloads.astro`, and the files in `news/`
back to the matching locations under `src/pages/`, then uncomment their links in
`src/data/site.ts`, `src/components/Header.astro`, and `src/components/Footer.astro`.
